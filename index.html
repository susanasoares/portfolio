How I Lead

Establish conditions that make quality repeatable and scalable.

Vision
Build towards a shared vision

Define the future state in a way that is inspiring, ambitious, and actionable.

Value
Align around customer value

Anchor decisions in the outcomes users need, not the features teams want to ship.

Simplicity
Simplify the experience

Reduce complexity until the product feels intuitive, useful, and easy to trust.

Craft
Obsess over the details

Hold the line on flows, content, interaction, hierarchy, edge cases, and handoff quality.

Systems
Codify the craft standard

Turn strong decisions into principles, patterns, and rituals that help quality scale.
