Case Study 02 Platform

MyDisney Identity

Unifying identity across Disney's global ecosystem without losing what makes each brand distinct.

Role
Director, Product Design
Team
5 Product Designers, 1 Content Designer, 1 Researcher
Scope
TWDC ecosystem; Identity, Commerce, and Platform UX
Overview

MyDisney was Disney's unified identity platform. This was a multi-year, cross-company initiative. I led design across Identity, Commerce, and Platform UX, partnering closely with Product, Engineering, Brand, Marketing, Legal, and executive leaders across Disney.

The challenge was to create a single identity experience that connected relationships across the Disney ecosystem while preserving the credibility and distinctiveness of each brand.
